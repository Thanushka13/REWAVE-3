⚠ WATER SAFETY & VALIDATION

Basic filtration alone cannot guarantee microbiological safety. Recovered water must be appropriately treated and tested for its intended reuse. Unsuitable water must be diverted rather than reused, and non-potable plumbing must remain separate from drinking-water systems. REWAVE is a student innovation concept, not a certified water-treatment system.
