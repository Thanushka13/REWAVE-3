⚠ WATER SAFETY & VALIDATION

Basic filtration alone cannot guarantee microbiological safety. Recovered water must receive appropriate treatment and testing for its intended reuse. Unsuitable water must be diverted rather than reused, and non-potable plumbing must remain separate from drinking-water systems. REWAVE is a student innovation concept, not a certified treatment system.
